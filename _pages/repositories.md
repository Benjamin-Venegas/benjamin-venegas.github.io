[Construction of dual spaces with respect to an isomorphism](/assets/pdf/Duality%20with%20respect%20to%20isomorphisms.pdf)
This short notes describes a construction related with extrapolated spaces/spaces of negative norm/Gelfand triples, in which one uses three spaces and one isomorphism  to constructs a new space and isomorphism. This new space identifies isometrically with a true dual, and allows for a special extension of the relevant isomorphism.
